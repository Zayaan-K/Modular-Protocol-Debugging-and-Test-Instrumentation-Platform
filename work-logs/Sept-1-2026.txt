Route the high-priority USB path and establish its uninterrupted ground reference. Do not route the entire board yet.

1. Placement readiness check

Confirm before routing:

Connector orientation and pad numbering match the datasheets.
The ESD array is immediately beside the USB female connector.
D+ and D− components are placed symmetrically.
The selected stack-up and provisional 90 Ω geometry are documented.
The route can remain on one layer without vias.
No mounting holes, plane cutouts or other traces obstruct the pair.



2.  Review how KiCad routes differential pairs and how your fabricator specifies controlled impedance. 

Differential width versus pair spacing
Keeping the same reference plane throughout the route
Why a ground-plane break disrupts the return-current path
Differential-pair skew and when length tuning is necessary
Clearance around connector pads and the ESD footprint
Whether your fabricator wants impedance-controlled nets identified in fabrication notes


3. Route D+ and D−

Route the pair using KiCad’s differential-pair router:

USB male → ESD array  → 0 Ω links → USB female

Follow these rules:

Remain on the top layer.
Use no vias unless physically unavoidable.
Keep width and spacing constant.
Use gentle 45° bends.
Keep both traces coupled for nearly the entire path.
Do not add branches to J3 or remote test points.
Keep any probe pads small, symmetric and directly in the route.
Avoid excessive length tuning; a short, direct pair is preferable to unnecessary meanders.
Allow only the unavoidable uncoupled regions at connector, resistor and ESD pads.

If the ESD package forces severe neck-down or crossed routing, reconsider its orientation or footprint.

4. Create and inspect the ground plane 

Add a solid ground zone on the layer directly beneath the USB pair—ideally L2 in the four-layer stack-up.

Inspect the entire route and verify:

Ground exists continuously beneath both traces.
There are no plane splits, keepouts or cutouts beneath the pair.
No unrelated signal crosses through its return-path region.
ESD ground connects to the plane with a very short path and nearby ground via arrangement.
Connector shield treatment matches your documented strategy.
USB signal ground remains continuous between connectors.


5. Run physical checks and document 

Run KiCad’s Design Rules Checker, then inspect the pair manually.

Document:

Total approximate pair length
Reported D+/D− length difference
Routing width and gap
Reference plane
Number of vias
Any unavoidable neck-down sections
Whether the geometry still needs fabricator confirmation

Use the 3D viewer once more to check connector alignment and exposed test-point access.
